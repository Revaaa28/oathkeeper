OATHKEEPER
waking the agent…

An agent that can prove it obeyed you.

Give an AI agent money and you must give it rules — a spending cap, a list of forbidden categories. Publish those rules and attackers get a map. Hide them and nobody can verify the agent obeyed. OATHKEEPER ends the dilemma: the rules are sealed into a cryptographic commitment, and every decision carries a zero-knowledge proof, verified on zkVerify, that the decision is the one the sealed rules force.

Open the seal

Judges should not have to trust that a real policy ever existed. This deliberate owner reveal — a demo feature, off in production — discloses the sealed rules so you can audit every verdict above against them.

OATHKEEPER runs a Circom 2 circuit (516 constraints) with Groth16 proofs on BN128, generated and verified with snarkjs. Decision proofs are submitted to zkVerify through the Kurier API and polled to finalization. Verify any proof yourself: take /api/proof/:id and /api/vkey and check them with plain snarkjs — no trust in this server required.

Honest note: the Groth16 keys come from a local development powers-of-tau ceremony, the flow the official zkVerify tutorials document; a production deployment would use a multi-party ceremony. The circuit constraints — the part that makes a forbidden decision unprovable — are unaffected. Built for the zkVerify Agent Games, Wildcard track. Source on GitHub · #AgentGames
