THE RULES
SEALED — NEVER SHOWN

Proved,
not promised.

Give an AI agent money and you must give it rules — a spending cap, a list of forbidden categories. Publish the rules and attackers get a map. Hide them and nobody can verify the agent obeyed. OATHKEEPER ends the dilemma: the rules are sealed into one cryptographic commitment, and every decision the agent makes carries a zero-knowledge proof — verified on zkVerify — that the decision is exactly the one the sealed rules force.
